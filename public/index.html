Introducing the game

Your teams don't win money. They win customers.

The Customer Journey Game© is an interactive customer journey simulation that uses experiential learning to deepen understanding of the end-to-end customer experience – and make it stick.

The board simulates every stage and touchpoint of a customer journey. Rather than hearing about the journey, teams live it – discovering through every roll, task and decision how each engagement strengthens or weakens the whole experience, and the role they play in customer loyalty and retention.

2 hoursFacilitated session
8 playersPer team, per game box
6 editionsTailored to your industry

How the simulation plays

Roll and move

Teams roll the dice and move their customer avatars through each stage of the journey.

Complete the tasks

Task blocks prompt teams to spot pain points, propose service improvements and work together.

Win happy customers

Collect Happy Customer tokens – and avoid the unhappy ones that lead to churn.

Crown the Customer Champion

The team with the most loyal customers wins the title.

In every game box 1 fabric game board · 72 game cards · 8 acrylic player pieces · Happy customer tokens · 1 large dice

Why experiential learning

People remember what they experience.

Customer experience is created at every touchpoint, by every department. By letting teams live the journey rather than just hear about it, the simulation builds deeper understanding – and lessons that are retained and applied long after the session ends.

Introducing TOUCHPOINTS

A smarter approach to building a customer-centric organisation.

TOUCHPOINTS is a workforce capability and enablement system that drives structured business improvement – powered by customer experience methodologies and thinking.

Rather than treating customer experience as the responsibility of one department, it brings people from across the organisation together to understand the customer journey, identify opportunities for improvement and take shared ownership of delivering better experiences.

What it combines

Practical CX learning

Workshops and tailored online learning that build skills people use in their roles.

Collaborative workshops

Cross-functional journey mapping and service blueprinting sessions.

Employee engagement

Internal CX Champions who involve colleagues in shaping improvement.

Digital improvement system

One place to identify pain points, recommend improvements and track progress.

Our primary objective

To build and embed a customer-centric culture that perpetually renews itself – empowering employees to deliver on the brand promise every day and creating a frictionless customer journey.

Empowering People

Stronger capability, confidence and a voice in improvement.

Improving Experiences

Smoother journeys for customers and colleagues alike.

Driving Business Performance

Operational improvement and measurable business outcomes.

The TOUCHPOINTS Infinity Loop

A self-perpetuating model of continuous improvement.

Capability and improvement feed each other in one continuous loop. Every improvement creates new learning, and every new skill drives further improvement – with CX Champions at the centre, connecting people and action.

Build capability

Pilot + 12-month programme

  1. Assess – your CX maturity baseline
  2. Discover – key journeys, roles and pain points
  3. Develop – a core team of CX Champions
  4. Enable – role-based learning for every employee

Drive improvement

TOUCHPOINTS Improvement System

  1. Identify & recommend – pain points and improvements
  2. Allocate – tasks with clear ownership
  3. Act – teams collaborate to resolve
  4. Measure – results feed back into learning

How it works

Three connected parts. One self-perpetuating system.

A select team is developed first, that team carries capability to the whole workforce, and a digital system turns everyone's participation into action. Each cycle feeds the next, creating a model of continuous improvement that sustains itself.

01 · 3-month pilot programme

Develops a select team of CX Champions

6–24 employees from across departments, functions and levels gain the knowledge, methods and tools to lead.

→ Champions become ambassadors

02 · 12-month programme

Enables the wider workforce

Champions act as stewards of the rollout, working with management to bring CX learning to every employee.

→ Employees participate through the Improvement System

03 · Improvement System

Connects participation to operational action

Employees identify pain points and recommend improvements; ownership is assigned and progress tracked to delivery.

↺ Progress and outcomes feed back
