What You’ll Learn

The Customer Experience Specialist CXS online course is structured into five expert-developed modules, covering every critical aspect of customer experience:

  • Strategy in Action – Develop and execute CX strategies that drive measurable business impact.
  • Culture and Accountability – Build a strong, customer-first culture across your organization.
  • Metrics and Analysis – Measure CX success with key data insights and analytics.
  • Improvement and Design – Use design thinking to continuously refine and enhance customer experiences.
  • Voice of the Customer & Intelligence – Leverage customer insights to shape strategic decisions

As a certified Customer Experience Specialist (CXS)™, you’ll be equipped to:

  • Turn customer insights into clear, strategic actions
  • Improve experiences that increase loyalty, retention, and revenue
  • Influence internal stakeholders with the right CX frameworks and metrics
  • Lead CX initiatives at any level, without needing a fancy title
