CXS certification

What you’ll learn

Both CXS pathways cover five areas of CX practice. Apply the learning to turn customer insights into measurable improvements and bring colleagues into the work.

  • Set a CX strategy and put it into practice.
  • Build a customer-focused culture with clear accountability.
  • Choose meaningful measures and analyse results.
  • Design and improve customer experiences.
  • Use customer feedback and evidence to guide decisions.

Choose the right course for your goals

The courses are suited to CX practitioners, marketing and customer success leaders, consultants and business owners who want to improve how their organisations serve customers.
