Your brand makes a promise. Is your business designed to deliver on it?

THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Your customers experience one brand. But does your business operate as one?

Your marketing promises convenience. Your frontline teams promise personal service. But when systems fail, information doesn't flow or departments work in silos, customers experience the gaps.

These aren't always customer service problems. They're business design problems — and no amount of asking employees to smile more will fix them.

01

Retail

The assistant is helpful. The advertised product isn't in stock. The brand promise breaks at inventory.

02

Fast food

The cashier gets the order right. The kitchen prepares it incorrectly. The promise breaks at the handoff.

03

Personal services

The technician is excellent. The booking system double-books the appointment. The promise breaks behind the scenes.

The customer doesn't see your departments. They experience the consequences of how well they work together.

THE BIG IDEA

The experience customers see is only half the story.

Every customer-facing moment depends on a network of people, processes, technology and decisions that customers rarely see. If those elements aren't connected, the experience becomes inconsistent — regardless of how committed your employees are.

THE KEYNOTE EXPERIENCE

In this engaging keynote, Lynn Baker takes audiences beyond traditional customer service thinking. Through relatable examples and a live demonstration of TOUCHPOINTS Blueprint Studio, she reveals how service blueprinting connects the brand promise to what must happen across the organisation to deliver it.

Audiences see how a single customer journey can expose hidden handoffs, unclear ownership, operational friction and improvement opportunities — and why consistent customer experiences must be designed, not left to chance.
