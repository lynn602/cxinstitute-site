The big idea

It looks like a board game. In reality, it is a reflection of your customer's journey.

This powerful combination brings together Lynn's inspirational keynote and the power of interaction with The Customer Journey Game — a business simulation that turns customer experience theory into lived experience.

Instead of being told what customers feel, your audience feels it. They move through awareness, consideration, purchase, experience, and loyalty and advocacy — encountering the same friction, delays, disconnects and delights your real customers meet every day.

Because the insight is earned rather than presented, it sticks. Teams leave with a shared language for customer experience and a shared understanding of how their own decisions ripple outward to the customer.

Ideal for conferences that need more than a talk, and for culture programmes that need experience-led learning people actually remember.

How the session runs

Inspire · Experience · Act.

Step 01

The keynote sets the scene.

Lynn opens with the inspirational 'Welcome to the Age of Experience' keynote — demystifying customer experience, showing how it differs from customer service and why every moment counts commercially.

Step 02

The game brings it to life.

Teams then play The Customer Journey Game — a business simulation that looks like a board game but is, in reality, a reflection of your customer's journey. Audiences stop listening and start experiencing.

Step 03

The debrief drives action.

A facilitated debrief converts the experience into insight: what your customers feel, where your journey leaks value and what each team can change immediately.
