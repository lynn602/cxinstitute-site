About the keynote

Where Business Strategy, Customer Experience and Artificial Intelligence converge.

Artificial Intelligence is changing everything. The organisations that win won't be the ones with the best AI. They'll be the ones that become more human because of it.— Lynn Baker

The future should not be about choosing between Artificial Intelligence and people. It should be about exploring how we bring them together to create exceptional humanized customer experiences.

Humanization is a framework that combines Business Strategy, Customer Experience and Artificial Intelligence to help organisations consider how they can create customer experiences that are more personal, meaningful and valuable.

In this thought-provoking keynote, Lynn Baker challenges the myth that AI will replace people. Instead, she shows how AI should be used to remove repetitive tasks, deliver intelligent insights and give employees more time to do what only humans can — build trust, show empathy, solve problems and create lasting relationships.

Used well, AI isn't a threat. It's a great opportunity that enables organisations to unlock human potential, strengthen customer relationships and drive loyalty, retention and sustainable growth.

This isn't a keynote about technology. It's about the future of customer relationships.

Why this keynote matters

Today's customers don't compare you to your competitors. They compare you to the best experience they've ever had.

They expect organisations to know them, understand them, respond quickly and deliver personalised, effortless experiences. Yet many employees spend more time on administration than building customer relationships.

Humanization shows how Artificial Intelligence can automate routine work, provide intelligent insights and give employees something they've never had enough of — time.

Time to listen. Time to solve problems. Time to build trust. Time to create exceptional humanized customer experiences that drive loyalty, retention and advocacy.

That's the real return on Artificial Intelligence.

01 · The Customer of the Future

Customer expectations have fundamentally evolved.

Discover why organisations must rethink the way they create value — and what future customers expect:

  • Hyper-personalization
  • Micro-moments that matter
  • High levels of transparency
  • Convenience & speed
  • Trust, empathy and compassion
  • Seamless experiences across every channel

02 · Every Moment Is An Experience

Customers don't remember processes. They remember micro-moments.

Customer Experience has evolved — from journeys, to touchpoints, to micro-moments. Every conversation. Every email. Every phone call. Every WhatsApp. Every promise. Every interaction.

  • Every moment matters.

03 · The Humanization Framework

A practical framework every organisation can immediately apply.

REFRAME

Reframe the value you create.

Stop selling products and services. Start communicating the value, impact and outcomes you create for your customers.

REALIGN

Realign how you deliver.

Align Business Strategy, people, processes, technology and culture around one shared purpose: consistently delivering on your brand and customer promise. Customers don't experience departments. They experience one organisation.

RETAIN

Retain — the intelligent way.

Use Artificial Intelligence to generate meaningful customer insights, then turn those insights into proactive conversations, personalized recommendations and proof of value that strengthens trust and deepens relationships. Technology doesn't replace relationships. It strengthens them.
