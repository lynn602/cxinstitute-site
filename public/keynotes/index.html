01

Welcome to the Age of Experience

Growth Without Discounting: Leading a Customer-Led Business in a Tight Economy

Welcome to the Age of Experience is an inspirational, engaging and informative introduction to the process, value and commercial power of customer experience.

Designed for audiences who want to understand CX and why it matters, this keynote demystifies customer experience, explains how it differs from customer service and reveals how everyday interactions influence customer acquisition, retention, loyalty, advocacy and sustainable business growth.

Audiences discover how the customer journey extends across five broad stages—Awareness, Consideration, Purchase, Experience, and Loyalty and Advocacy—and how each stage contains multiple touchpoints and micro-moments that shape the customer's perception of an organisation.

B2B Customer Experience Reimagined · Client Partner Experience (CPX)

Frequently asked

Booking Lynn, answered.

How long is a typical keynote?

The flagship keynote runs 45–60 minutes. There is also a 120 minute session that combines the keynote with The Customer Journey Game, plus half-day and full-day workshop formats.

Who should be in the room?

Every person, at every level. Executives, managers, sales teams, customer service professionals, contact centre advisors, operations and support staff all leave with practical ideas they can apply immediately.

Is the content tailored to our industry?

Wherever possible, keynotes are shaped around the client's industry, audience and business context. If time allows, Lynn recommends that prior to the conference, attendees complete a short Sentiment Survey beforehand, so that she can customise the content based on audience needs, challenges and current sentiment.

Do delegates get anything to take away?

Yes. Every keynote includes a pre-event briefing and is tailored to your audience, industry and event objectives. Relevant keynote resources, assessments or practical takeaways are included according to the selected keynote experience. For the Welcome to the World of Humanization keynote, that includes the Humanization Sentiment Survey and the Humanization Playbook with 18 practical Quick Win actions.

Does Lynn travel internationally?

Yes. Lynn is based in Johannesburg and has delivered keynotes across South Africa, the Middle East, Europe and beyond. Virtual and hybrid sessions are also available.

What are Lynn's speaker fees?

Fees depend on format, duration, audience size and location. Ask for a proposal through the enquiry form below.
