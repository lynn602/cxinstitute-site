Lynn Baker Full Bio

Lynn Baker is a customer experience strategist and international keynote speaker based in Johannesburg, South Africa. Over more than 25 years, she has helped organisations connect the way they serve customers to the results they want to achieve, from customer retention and loyalty to long-term growth.

She is the founder and CEO of the Customer Experience Institute South Africa (CX Institute SA), the South African partner of CX University. She trained at the Disney Institute, served for six years as Chair of Judges for the International Customer Experience Awards and was named among the Top 50 Women in CX. She created The Customer Journey Game, an interactive business simulation, and developed the Client Partner Experience (CPX) framework for B2B organisations.

Lynn speaks across South Africa, the Middle East, Europe and the UK. Her keynotes draw on storytelling, humour, business examples and live audience participation to help teams apply customer experience thinking to strategy, sales, leadership and change.
